Support on campus

Counseling, food, emergency money, disability services and more. Part of Support and your situation, for graduate school and law school.

Applications are stressful, and so is everything else that happens in college. Queens College has free help for your mind, your money, food and more.

  • Counseling. Counseling Services are free and confidential for current students: Frese Hall, first floor, 718-997-5420, CounselingServices@qc.cuny.edu. After hours, call the same number and press 1 to reach a licensed clinician.
  • In a crisis. Call or text 988, text CUNY to 741741 any time, or call 911.
  • Food. The Knights Table Food Pantry, Student Union lower level, Room 29, is free for current QC and CUNY students; book a visit online, once every two weeks. CUNY also helps students apply for SNAP.
  • Emergency money. The Student Emergency Grant gives up to $3,000 over your time at QC for things like rent, utilities, medical bills or lost income (not tuition). Email PetrieGrant@qc.cuny.edu.
  • Disability services. The Office for Students with Disabilities and Access (formerly Special Services), Kiely Hall 104, 718-997-5870. Register through CUNY Accommodate and request accommodations each semester; it can take up to two weeks, so start early.
  • Health. Health Services, temporarily in the Student Union, Room 327, 718-997-2760.
  • LGBTQ students. The LGBTQIAA+ Programs and Resource Center, Queens Hall 232.
  • Problems with a class or grade. The Office of Student Advocacy and Appeals, Frese Hall 201, handles appeals and late withdrawals. The Dean of Students runs a Care and Concern Team.
  • Immigrant students. See Undocumented and DACA students.
