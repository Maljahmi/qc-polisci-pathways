Applying to PhD programs

Fees, waivers, the GRE and the rest of the file. Part of Apply, for graduate school.

Political science PhD applications are due from late November to December 17 for fall entry (Columbia's is Nov 25), and the APSA Diversity Fellowship closes earlier, on October 25, 2026.

Application fees ($75–$125 each) and who waives them

School (fee)Waives for these programsOtherwise

Program not on the list? Email the department's graduate coordinator and ask anyway, in October rather than the week of the deadline.

The rest of the file

Letters (usually 3)From faculty who have seen your research or writing. Ask 6–8 weeks ahead: see Recommendation letters.
Statement of purposeLead with the question you want to study, show your preparation, and name 2–3 faculty per school whose work fits.
Writing sampleYour best research paper, often a seminar paper or thesis chapter, cut to the page limit and revised with a professor.
Where to applyFit with faculty matters more than rank. Look for people whose recent papers you would want to have written.
