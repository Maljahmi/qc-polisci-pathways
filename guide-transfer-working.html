Transfer and working students

Transfer credits, work-study, flexible courses and internships for credit. Part of Support and your situation, for graduate school and law school.

If you transferred to Queens College or work while you study, you have less time on campus to meet professors and find programs. These steps help you make the most of it.

Transferring in

  • Transfer advising. The Academic Advising Center in Kiely Hall 217; book on QC Navigate or email advising@qc.cuny.edu.
  • Your credits. See your transfer credit evaluation in CUNYfirst. Any passing grade from a CUNY college transfers (C- or better from elsewhere), and you must earn at least 30 credits at QC. If a course came in wrong, ask the department to sign a re-evaluation form. CUNY Transfer Explorer shows how courses transfer between CUNY colleges.
  • Meet one professor in your first semester. Letters of recommendation and research jobs come from professors who know you, so start early. Talk to your professors.

Working while you study

  • Federal Work-Study. File the FAFSA (QC's code is 002690) and take at least 6 credits. Jobs pay $18 an hour on campus and $19 off campus, up to 20 hours a week, and are posted on Handshake. If it isn't in your aid package, email qcworkstudy@qc.cuny.edu to join the waitlist. Work-Study
  • Flexible courses. ePermit lets you take a course at another CUNY college, including online sections (you need a 2.0 GPA and a QC equivalent); winter and spring 2027 ePermits open October 21, 2026. CUNY Online lists more than 220 online options.
  • Short paid projects. Parker Dewey micro-internships are paid projects for real companies, and The Forage has free, self-paced job simulations.
  • Remote and part-time programs. Some programs on this site fit around a job, like the Wilson Center's remote research assistants and the U.S.-China Relations internships.

Internships for credit

  • Political science internship courses. Earn credit while you intern: PSCI 2983 and 2986 (independent internships, 120 or 240 hours), PSCI 2925 (urban politics), PSCI 2954 (law and advocacy), PSCI 294 (a full semester in Albany, with a stipend) and PSCI 299 (a summer in Washington). Apply through the department at least a month before registration. Political science internships
  • Get paid for an unpaid internship. Most of these internships are unpaid. QC's internship stipend gives $2,700 once; applications for 2026–27 open in November 2026.
