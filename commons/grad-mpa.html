What an MPA is

A Master of Public Administration teaches you to manage public and nonprofit organizations: budgets, operations and people. CUNY's MPA programs cost far less than private ones.

  • Length: 1–2 years
  • Cost: Baruch Marxe and John Jay charge NY residents $6,375 a semester full-time; Columbia SIPA tuition is $74,220 a year
  • Pick it if: You want to run programs, agencies or nonprofits
  • Prepare: City, state and nonprofit internships, then fellowships like Coro and the CUNY Civil Service Pathways Fellowship

MPA timeline

Future MPA students build a record of public service: city, state and nonprofit work, then a fellowship or a city job.
