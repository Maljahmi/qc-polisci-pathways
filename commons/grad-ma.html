What an MA is

A master's in political science gives you graduate-level political science and methods without committing to a PhD. It helps if your record needs strengthening first. A master's in methods or data is another route.

  • Length: 1–2 years
  • CUNY: Graduate Center (30 credits, master's thesis); Brooklyn College (30 credits, evening and online); City College offers a Master of International Affairs instead
  • Private: UChicago MAPSS (one year; 73% of graduates who applied to PhDs got funded offers) and Columbia cost far more
  • Methods or data: QC's own Data Analytics programs lead to an MA, with a faster 4+1 route for sociology majors. The Columbia SPS CUNY Fellowship fully funds some Columbia master's for CUNY students
  • Pick it if: You need more methods before a PhD, or want a data career now

MA timeline

An MA works as a bridge to a PhD or a data career. The QC route is the 4+1, which needs a sociology major and a 3.5 GPA by junior spring.

A paid city job with professional development and a path into a permanent civil service title. Apply in your last year.

  • Who: CUNY graduates living in the five boroughs; US work authorization
  • Pay: $48,956 in most tracks. The January 2027 cohort closed Sept 4, 2026

EPIK (English Program in Korea)

When: Rolling · spring intake through January
Opens: Year-round

Seniors and recent grads · South Korean public schools

Korean government jobs abroad teaching English in public schools, with housing provided.

  • Who: US citizens (and a few other countries) with a bachelor's; a 100-hour TEFL certificate if your degree isn't related
  • Pay: About ₩2.35–2.8 million a month, housing and a settlement allowance

Peace Corps

When: Rolling · each opening has its own date
Opens: Year-round

Seniors and recent grads · 60+ countries · 2 years and 3 months

Two years of community service abroad, with a living allowance and graduate school benefits afterward.

  • Who: US citizens 18 or older, usually with a four-year degree
  • Pay: A living allowance, housing, medical care, travel and a readjustment allowance at the end

State Department Consular Fellows Program

When: Any time · the test is offered on demand

Seniors and recent grads · U.S. embassies and consulates abroad · up to five years

A paid job abroad deciding visa cases and helping U.S. citizens, for people who speak Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, Portuguese or Spanish well. It is not a faster way into the Foreign Service.

  • Who: U.S. citizens, at least 20 to apply, who speak one of the four languages and can get a Top Secret clearance; dual nationals can't serve in their other country
  • Pay: A federal salary (not posted) with tax-free housing overseas; the test costs $5, refunded when you show up
