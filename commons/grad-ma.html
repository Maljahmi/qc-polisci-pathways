What an MA is

A master's in political science gives you graduate-level political science and methods without committing to a PhD. It helps if your record needs strengthening first. A master's in methods or data is another route.

  • Length: 1–2 years
  • CUNY: Graduate Center (30 credits, master's thesis); Brooklyn College (30 credits, evening and online); City College offers a Master of International Affairs instead
  • Private: UChicago MAPSS (one year; 73% of graduates who applied to PhDs got funded offers) and Columbia cost far more
  • Methods or data: QC's own Data Analytics programs lead to an MA, with a faster 4+1 route for sociology majors. The Columbia SPS CUNY Fellowship fully funds some Columbia master's for CUNY students
  • Pick it if: You need more methods before a PhD, or want a data career now

Your timeline

An MA works as a bridge to a PhD or a data career. The QC route is the 4+1, which needs a sociology major and a 3.5 GPA by junior spring.

QC Scholarships, General Application

Deadline: Oct 25, 2026 · spring cycle mid-Jan to mid-Mar

All years · QC Foundation scholarships · one application, every semester

One general application on QC's AcademicWorks portal puts you in the running for most of the college's donor scholarships, based on merit, need, major or class year. Fall applications pay for the spring term, and spring applications pay for the fall. A few awards add a short supplemental question at the end.

  • Who: Matriculated QC students with a QC email, including second-degree students. The office says most awards are open to students without documentation
  • For political science: Minister Jason Peck ($500; preference for political science majors and minors), Sig and Mary Hecht (financial need and a 3.0+ GPA in a social science), Swimmer (preference for students interested in law) and Brownstein/McDermott ($5,000; political theory counts; 30+ credits and a 3.5+ GPA)
  • Tip: Apply every semester. The office suggests listing a reference (email them first) and any community service

Accelerated BA/MA in Data Analytics and Applied Social Research

When: 4+1 · BA + MA at QC

Sophomores and juniors · QC Sociology · four graduate courses count toward both degrees

Finish a master's in data analytics about a year after your BA. It is open only to sociology majors, so political science majors get in by double majoring in sociology. The MA prepares graduates for market research, program evaluation, public opinion, public health and applied social research jobs.

  • Who: Sociology majors, double majors included; 3.5 overall GPA; B+ or better in DATA 205 and DATA 212
  • How: Talk to sociology professors early about whether the 4+1 fits your plan, then apply through QC's Accelerated Degrees system. You can opt out at any time

Sophomores and juniors · 21-credit honors minor · QC

A research course (HSS 350) and a thesis colloquium (HSS 390). Political science has no separate honors thesis, so this is the thesis route.

  • Who: 3.3 GPA to take HSS 200; B+ or better in it to join
