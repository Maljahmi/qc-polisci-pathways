A student-made guide for Queens College Political Science majors headed to graduate school or law school: paid research programs, internships, fellowships, deadlines and sample résumés, with every program linked to its official page.

Choose a path

  • Graduate school PhD, predoc, MPP, MPA and MA: funded PhDs, paid research jobs and policy master's degrees, with a timeline for each year of college.
  • Law school The JD: the LSAT and fee waivers, pre-law programs, legal internships and gap years.

How it works

Pick your path, then a degree. Each degree page explains the degree, gives a timeline for each year of college, and lists the programs that prepare you for it, sorted by next deadline. The Guides pages cover professors, methods, applying and résumés.

  • Good programs pay you. Almost everything here is free and comes with a stipend, housing, or both.
  • Deadlines move every year. Confirm on the official page before you plan around a date.
  • Ask for help. Professors, QC Pre-Law Advising and the Office of Honors and Scholarships are here to help you apply.
