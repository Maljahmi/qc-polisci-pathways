Choosing a graduate degree

A political science degree leads to several kinds of graduate programs. Pick one to see what it is, what to do each year of college, and the programs that help you get there. Not sure yet? Most of these programs exist to help you find out.

  • PhD: Research and teaching. Funded, 5–6 years
  • MPP: Design and evaluate policy. About 2 years
  • MPA: Run public and nonprofit organizations. 1–2 years
  • MA: Graduate study and methods, no PhD required. 1–2 years
  • Guides: Professors, methods, applying and résumés
