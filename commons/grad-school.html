Choosing a graduate degree

A political science degree leads to several kinds of graduate programs. Open one to see what it is and what it takes. Not sure yet? Most of the programs on this site exist to help you find out.

  • PhD Research and teaching. Funded, 5–6 years
  • Predoc A paid research job before a PhD. Any major, 1–2 years
  • MPP Design and evaluate policy. About 2 years
  • MPA Run public and nonprofit organizations. 1–2 years
  • MA Graduate study and methods, no PhD required. 1–2 years
  • Guides Professors, methods, applying, scholarships and job search tools

Hello! I made this site because, as an undergrad, I didn't know about all the opportunities I had. It's based on my own experience, so it won't match everyone's. Questions or comments? Email me at mohamed.aljahmi@qc.cuny.edu. I'm always happy to help and can point you to the right resources on campus.
