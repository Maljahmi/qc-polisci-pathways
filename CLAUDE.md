# Queens College Political Science Pathways

A static website for Queens College (CUNY) political science majors: year by year, what to do, how to do it and what to apply to, for graduate school, law school and jobs. Author: Mohamed Aljahmi (student). The site is student-made, not an official Queens College publication, and the footer says so. Keep that disclaimer.

The current design went live Oct 17, 2026 (made with the Taste skill, `design-taste-frontend` in `~/.claude/skills`). The old single-page site is kept in `classic/`, which is still the source of the guide text.

## Files

- **Pages at the root.**
  - `index.html` (hand-written): the home page. Hero, the goal picker ("Aiming for" PhD, Predoc, MPP, MPA, MA, Law school), an eight-question quiz for students who aren't sure (`quiz.js`), the year-by-year path (each year: what to do, how to do it, what to apply to), the About panel with Mohamed's note, and the footer. It loads `oldlinks.js` first, so links to the old site (`index.html#g-phd`, `#gp-truman-scholarship`) open the right new page.
  - Generated by `tools/build_site.py`: `grad.html`, `law.html`, `jobs.html`, `programs.html` (search, filters, grouped by month; `programs.html#id` opens a program), `guides.html` plus one `guide-*.html` per guide, `about.html`, `plans.js` (the timelines as data) and `oldlinks.js`. Never edit these by hand; edit the source and rebuild.
  - `handout.html` (hand-written) and `handout.js`: a one-page summary of each path (`handout.html?path=grad` or `?path=law`), black and white so it prints on any printer (Mohamed, Oct 2026). It has no live deadlines, only "When things usually close" (the months programs usually close), so it doesn't go out of date; a QR code (`assets/qr-programs-*.svg`, made by `tools/make_qr.py` and checked with a QR reader) opens Programs. Its year-by-year text is a short version of the timelines, so update both together. It must fit one letter page: check that `#page` is no taller than 1056px at 8.5in wide.
  - `downloads/Pathways_Graduate_School_Handout.pdf` and `..._Law_School_Handout.pdf`: the digital versions to email, made from `handout.html?path=grad&pdf=1` (the same page with links to the site; site links carry `utm_source=handout-pdf`, and the QR codes `utm_source=handout`, so analytics can count them). After changing the handout, regenerate both with Edge: `msedge --headless=new --no-pdf-header-footer --virtual-time-budget=15000 --print-to-pdf=downloads/Pathways_Graduate_School_Handout.pdf "http://localhost:8000/handout.html?path=grad&pdf=1"` (and `path=law`, `Law`).
- **Scripts and styles.** `common.js` (every page: counts, the "last updated" line, entrances, résumé tabs, links to other sites opening in a new tab), `path-core.js` (the year-by-year path, its guide links per year and goal), `path.js` (home), `quiz.js`, `subject.js` (graduate and law pages), `guide.js` (program lists on guides and the Jobs page), `programs.js`. `taste.css` (home and shared), `pages.css` (inside pages). Photos in `assets/` (grayscale Wikimedia Commons; credits in `assets/credits.json`, shown on `about.html#credits`).
- `data.js`: the opportunities database (`window.PROGRAMS`), one entry per program, plus `window.ALIASES`, `window.EXTRA_DEADLINES` and `window.UPDATED`. Edit programs here.
- `classic/`: the old single-page site (`index.html`, `app.js`, `styles.css`, `handout.html`), still working at /classic/ with a banner pointing to the current site and `noindex`. **`classic/index.html` is the content source**: the guides, degree descriptions and timelines are written there, and `tools/build_site.py` copies its sections into the new pages word for word (author's words included, untouched) and rewrites its `#id` links to the new pages.
- `downloads/`: the sample résumé, CV and law résumé as editable `.docx` files with `.pdf` copies.
- `commons/`: an optional WordPress version for the CUNY Academic Commons (block code, `site.css` for Appearance → Simple CSS, and `README.md` with posting steps). Not the main site. The pages are Group blocks with `pp-…` classes laid out by `site.css`; Twenty Twenty wraps each Group in an inner container and caps widths at 58rem, so `site.css` overrides that with strong selectors. See `commons/README.md` → Layout.
- `tools/` (Python standard library only):
  - `build_site.py` builds the pages above from `classic/index.html` and `data.js`. Its `GUIDES` list (id, file, title, one line, path) and `GROUPS` decide which sections become guide pages; add a new section there. `LINKS` maps old ids to new pages. It must print "unknown link targets: none".
  - `build_commons.py` rebuilds `commons/` from `classic/index.html` and `data.js`. `PAGES` lists which sections go on each Commons page; update it when you add a section.
  - `build_docx.py` rebuilds the `.docx` files from the `.sheet` samples in `classic/index.html`; export the PDFs from Word afterward.

After content changes run `python tools/build_site.py` and `python tools/build_commons.py` (set `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8` in Git Bash). There is no framework and no dependencies; the only external resource is Google Fonts. `_mockup-taste/` is the old preview folder (git-ignored); it is out of date and nothing reads it.

## How the content is built

- **Last updated:** `window.UPDATED` in data.js (YYYY-MM-DD) fills the `[data-updated]` lines. The "checked in October 2026" lines (`<span data-checked>` on the About page and the classic footer, and the Commons footer when it is built) show the month and year of `window.UPDATED`, so they move to the new month on their own (Mohamed, Oct 2026). The weekly task checks every program link on its first run of each month to keep that true.
- **Weekly update:** a scheduled task in the Claude app (`qcpp-weekly-update`, Mondays 9 PM since Oct 19, 2026, when Mohamed's PC is usually on; it only runs while the Claude app is open) refreshes deadlines, adds verified programs, sets `window.UPDATED`, runs both builders and pushes to `main` (Mohamed chose automatic publishing). It stages only site files, never `_mockup*` or `.taste-skill/`. Anything left uncommitted on Monday evening gets published with it.
- **Links to other sites open in a new tab** (Mohamed, Oct 2026): `build_site.py` adds `target="_blank" rel="noopener"` to every outside link it writes, and `common.js` does the same for links drawn after load. Write hand-written outside links with both attributes.
- **Where things show up in the new pages:** each guide section becomes `guide-*.html`; the degree sections become panels on `grad.html`; the timelines become `plans.js` and the year-by-year path; `path-core.js` (`GUIDES`) picks four guides per year and goal; `guide.js` fills program lists from a `data-spec` filter (degrees, types, years, paid, limit).
- **The classic page's layout** (for editing the source): each path is a `<main>` (`home`, `grad`, `law`, `opportunities`) with tabs; Degrees are `<details class="deg">` (`g-phd`, `g-predoc`, `g-mpp`, `g-mpa`, `g-ma`); timelines are `g-timeline`, `g-predoc-timeline`, `g-mpp-timeline`, `g-mpa-timeline`, `g-ma-timeline` and `l-timeline`; guides sit in `<details class="grp">` groups: Get ready (`g-ready`), Apply (`g-applying`), Money and jobs (`g-money`); law has `l-ready` and `l-applying`.
- **Sections.** `<section id="g-…">` or `<section id="l-…">`. The header is `.sh` with an `h2`. There are no section numbers.
- **Links.** In `classic/index.html`, write links between sections and to programs as `href="#id"` (program ids are `gp-…` and `lp-…`); `build_site.py` turns them into links to the right new page. Add `data-type="summer-internship"` (or any type) to a link to a list, such as `#g-phd-db`, to open Programs filtered. Old law-path ids that duplicate a grad program are mapped in `window.ALIASES` in `data.js`.
- **Programs (`data.js`).** Each entry has:
  - `id`, `name` (HTML with the official link), `url`, and `short` (optional, the short name in lists)
  - `types`: any of `summer-research`, `summer-internship`, `fall-internship`, `spring-internship`, `fellowship`, `campus`, `visit`, `pipeline` (pre-law programs only), `course`, `after`, `journalism`. `journalism` is a field, not a format: put it last, after the format type, and the list then hides the degree label for that program. Journalism programs use degrees `mpp`, `mpa`, `ma` so they appear in the graduate school list
  - `years`: any of `fr`, `so`, `jr`, `sr`, `grad` (after college); `degrees`: any of `phd`, `predoc`, `mpp`, `mpa`, `ma`, `jd`
  - `cost` (optional; used for pre-law programs since Oct 19, 2026): `funded` (free, and travel, housing or meals for any in-person part are covered), `free` (free, but you pay your own travel or housing), or `fee` (it charges students; put the amount in a `Cost` fact). Lists show "Fully funded", "Free" or "Charges a fee" before the type, and the "Paid or fully funded" filter counts `funded`. A program that pays the student uses `paid: true` instead
  - `paid` (`true`, `false`, or `null` if it doesn't apply) and `allOpen` (`true` only if the official page says DACA, undocumented or international students can apply)
  - Dates: `whenB` and `whenS` are the big and small date text. `due` is this cycle's deadline (ISO); it adds a countdown. `status` is `confirmed`, `estimate`, `last` (only last cycle's date is posted) or `none` (rolling or TBA). `dueMD` is the typical deadline as `MM-DD`, used for sorting. `opens` and `opensMD` are the opening date text and `MM-DD`. Set `event: true` for a date that is an event, not a deadline.
  - Text: `meta` (where, length), `body`, `warn`, and `facts` as `[label, html]` pairs such as `["Pay", "$20 an hour"]`.
  - Lists sort by next deadline: this cycle's `due` if it's still ahead, otherwise the next `dueMD`. When a new cycle posts, update `due`, `status`, `whenB` and `whenS`.
- **Heading levels.** Screen readers navigate by heading, so never skip a level. In the source, group subheads are `<h3 class="kick">`, and a program row written in a section is an `h3` under the section's `h2`, or an `h4` under a `.kick`. `build_site.py` demotes headings one level inside guide pages.
- **Other blocks** in the source: `.bento` (Pair your major), `.plan` (a year-by-year timeline: one `li.plan-yr` per year with a `.plan-head` and three `.term` blocks of short items; mark the busiest year with `key` and a `.plan-tag`), `.talk` (advice block with a sample email or prompts), `.sheet` (sample résumés and CV), `.tbl` (tables; the jobs table stacks into blocks on phones), `.pull`, `.note`, `.note-from`.

## Design rules (from the author)

- **Brand:** the site follows the Queens College brand: the Brand Graphics Guidelines and Website Branding Guidelines at qc.cuny.edu/communications/queens-college-branding. Do not use the QC logo or the Q-and-swoosh mark. They are trademarked, need approval from the Office of Communications, and this guide is unofficial.
- **Fonts:** Libre Baskerville (the free web version of ITC New Baskerville, QC's primary typeface) for headings, the author's note and the About text; Cabin (standing in for Gill Sans, QC's secondary typeface) for everything else. No Geist, Inter, Roboto, Arial or Helvetica, and no monospace. Five type sizes (tokens in `taste.css`); body text 17px.
- **Color:** QC Red `#E71939` (accent; small red text uses the darker `#B20E28` for contrast), QC black and white, light gray surfaces. Flat color only: no gradients, shadows or thin decorative rules (real tables keep their lines). Text on QC red must be pure white. Never set red text on a colored background (a QC rule).
- **Photos:** real Wikimedia Commons photos of Queens College, New York and government buildings, shown in black and white, with credits. Don't add stock photos.
- **Layout:** asymmetric (split columns, ledger rows, plain lists), square corners. No evenly spaced grids of identical cards and no centered hero.
- **Academic, not template-like (Mohamed, Sept 2026):** no highlighter marks, no section numbers, no decorative stripes or dots, and plain descriptive headlines instead of slogans ("Choosing a graduate degree," not "Want to pursue grad school? Great."). Mohamed rejected travel themes ("Departures") and slogan headlines ("229 ways in").
- **Motion:** a masked headline on load and entrances as things scroll in. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Background:** always the white theme, even in dark mode. No dark theme.
- **Mobile:** every page must work at 390px wide with no horizontal scroll. Check this after layout changes.
- **Checks:** `node <skill>/scripts/lint.mjs <folder>` and `node <skill>/scripts/inspect.mjs <url>` (Node 24 is installed through winget; see the laptop notes). Inside pages still get "few pictures", "no video" and "still page" flags meant for landing pages; those are accepted.

## Content rules

- **Links:** every program name links to its official page, not a third-party listing where avoidable.
- **Dates:** check each date on the official page before adding it.
  - If only an older date is posted, label it "last cycle".
  - Mark estimates with "~" or "est." in `whenB`/`whenS`, and set `status: "estimate"` in `data.js`.
- **Remove, don't warn:** take out programs that are discontinued, paused, grad-student-only, or not open to Queens College students.
- **Style:** American spelling. Short, plain sentences. No filler.
- **Name:** always "Queens College Political Science", never "QC poli sci".
- **Graduate school:** write "graduate school", never "grad school" (Mohamed, Oct 2026). Ids, classes and Commons page slugs such as `grad-school` stay as they are, so links keep working.
- **The author's own words:** the "Notes from RBSI" section, the `.note-from` notes (on the home page, the closing note in Building a network, `g-network`, and the "Remember: you can coast through a degree" note in Using AI the right way, `g-ai`, Oct 2026) and the About preface are Mohamed's words (lightly copyedited). Do not generate or rewrite them; change them only with him.
- **Sample documents:** they are for fictional students (Alex Rivera, Jordan Lee). Keep placeholders like `[Name]` for real people and places.
